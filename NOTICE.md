## Repository role

`GerhardBalz/pizza-ontology` is a preservation, stewardship, engineering, and learning environment derived from Pizza Ontology 2.0.

The repository does **not** claim to be:

- the original Pizza ontology source repository;
- the original ontology authority;
- the legal rights holder of the historical Pizza ontology;
- an officially sanctioned continuation of the historical `co-ode.org` identifier space.

The detailed provenance and authority analysis is documented in [docs/pizza-provenance.md](docs/pizza-provenance.md).
