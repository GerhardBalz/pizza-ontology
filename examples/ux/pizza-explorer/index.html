Interpretation

“Required topping” is deliberately precise

In this projection, a required topping represents an OWL existential hasTopping some X condition. It means at least one such topping is required; it is not a closed list of every topping the Pizza may have.

Human-friendly names derived from CURIE local names are presentation conveniences only. Semantic identity is carried by the CURIE and full IRI from the projection.

UI-specific layout, filtering, wording, and display formatting live here in the experience layer. They do not become Pizza ontology semantics.
